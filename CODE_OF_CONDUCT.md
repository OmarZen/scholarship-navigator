# Code of Conduct

Scholarship Navigator exists to open doors for students everywhere, whatever their country, language, background or
experience level. We want contributing to feel the same way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors and people writing in their second language.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain *why* and suggest a fix.
- Credit others' work and sources.
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's personal documents, grades or contact details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language (about nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender,
  disability, age, or anything else).
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, scams, or using the project to advertise paid "guaranteed scholarship" services.
- Knowingly adding false scholarship information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately via the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open a
private security advisory (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability") if it involves leaked personal data. Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in
public.
